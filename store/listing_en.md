# Google Play – English (add as a translation)

**App name (max 30):** My Little Puppy

**Short description (max 80):**
Care for your puppy, play, train tricks and watch it grow! 🐶

**Full description (max 4000):**
Pick a puppy and take care of it – from a tiny pup to a grown-up dog!

🍖 Feed it, 🛁 wash it, 🌙 let it sleep and 💊 heal it when it gets sick.
🎾 Play together: running, lane racing and a puzzle.
🐾 Teach tricks – sit, lie down, beg, paw, jump and spin. Draw each trick with your finger!
🏠 Spend the coins you earn on a doghouse, a bed, accessories and clothes.
🐕 Five different breeds: wolf, doberman, kuvasz, shepherd and bulldog – each one grows and looks different.

Simple, picture-based and with very little text – fun for young and old.
No ads, no in-app purchases, works offline.
